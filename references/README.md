# references

- [hutter2000-aixi](hutter2000-aixi-cs0004001.pdf): Hutter, *A Theory of Universal Artificial Intelligence based on Algorithmic Complexity* (2000). AIXI, the universally intelligent agent FINXI is named against.
- [leike2015-bad-universal-priors](leike2015-bad-universal-priors-1510.04931.pdf): Leike & Hutter, *Bad Universal Priors and Notions of Optimality* (2015). AIXI's optimality depends on the choice of universal Turing machine; AIXI is a relative theory.
- [solomonoff1964-formal-theory-inductive-inference-pt1](solomonoff1964-formal-theory-inductive-inference-pt1.pdf): Solomonoff, *A Formal Theory of Inductive Inference, Part I* (1964). The universal prior: an index over all strings by the lengths of the programs that generate them.
- Chaitin, *A Theory of Program Size Formally Identical to Information Theory*, Journal of the ACM 22(3):329–340 (1975). Prefix-free programs, which make the universal prior a proper (sub)probability, and the halting probability Ω, the uncomputable weight of all programs that halt. No PDF; not freely available.
- [schmidhuber2008-compression-progress](schmidhuber2008-compression-progress-0812.4360.pdf): Schmidhuber, *Driven by Compression Progress* (2008). The compression theory of creativity and fun, which FINXI answers with creation and comprehension.
- [lehman2011-abandoning-objectives](lehman2011-abandoning-objectives.pdf): Lehman & Stanley, *Abandoning Objectives: Evolution through the Search for Novelty Alone* (2011). Search without an objective, guided by novelty.
- [hughes2024-open-endedness](hughes2024-open-endedness-2406.04268.pdf): Hughes et al., *Open-Endedness is Essential for Artificial Superhuman Intelligence* (2024). Open-endedness defined relative to an observer, as producing artifacts that are both novel and learnable.
- [finzi2026-epiplexity](finzi2026-epiplexity-2601.03220.pdf): Finzi, Qiu, Jiang, Izmailov, Kolter, Wilson, *From Entropy to Epiplexity* (2026). Structure a computationally bounded observer can learn, and how computation creates it.
- [goodfellow2014-gan](goodfellow2014-gan-1406.2661.pdf): Goodfellow et al., *Generative Adversarial Networks* (2014). The GAN that GEN is named against.
- [sukhbaatar2017-asymmetric-self-play](sukhbaatar2017-asymmetric-self-play-1703.05407.pdf): Sukhbaatar et al., *Intrinsic Motivation and Automatic Curricula via Asymmetric Self-Play* (2017). One agent does something and the other must reproduce it; a teacher and learner that build a curriculum between them.
- [cowsik2026-self-play-zero-data](cowsik2026-self-play-zero-data-2609.30063.pdf): Cowsik, Dolev, Li, De Luca, Cohen, Goodman, Levine, *Self-Play Pretraining with Zero Data* (2026). A generator teaches a learner from zero data; the working example of a GEN.
- [wang2023-voyager](wang2023-voyager-2305.16291.pdf): Wang et al., *Voyager: An Open-Ended Embodied Agent with Large Language Models* (2023). An agent that saves, retrieves, and composes its own creations as a growing library.
